PUBLISH ONLINE AND SHARE ON WHATSAPP

This package is prepared for Render hosting. It includes a persistent disk for the
SQLite booking database, a secure generated app secret, and a WhatsApp share link
on the client booking page.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A GitHub account
- A Render account
- A computer to upload this project to GitHub

STEPS
1. Create/sign in to GitHub.
2. Create a new private repository, for example: coworking-conference-booking.
3. Upload the CONTENTS of this extracted folder to that repository (not the ZIP file).
4. Sign in to Render and choose New > Blueprint.
5. Connect GitHub and select your repository.
6. Render reads render.yaml and proposes the web service and persistent disk.
7. Enter a strong ADMIN_PASSWORD when Render asks for it. Use at least 10 characters.
8. Review the service plan and disk cost shown by Render, then approve deployment.
9. Wait for deployment to finish. Render will give you a public URL similar to
   https://your-service-name.onrender.com
10. Open that URL on your phone and test a sample booking.
11. Open https://your-service-name.onrender.com/admin/login and sign in with
    the ADMIN_PASSWORD you set.
12. Send the public booking URL to clients on WhatsApp. The client page also has
    a "Share booking page on WhatsApp" link.

IMPORTANT
- The public URL is the CLIENT link. Do not share /admin/login with customers.
- The database is stored on Render's persistent disk at /var/data/bookings.db.
- Do not delete the disk or service if you need to keep booking records.
- Check the actual plan and persistent-disk charges displayed by Render before
  approving. This configuration uses a paid service plan because the app needs
  an always-on service and persistent storage.
- Keep ADMIN_PASSWORD private. Use HTTPS (Render provides it for its public URL).
- Test bookings, cancellations, opening hours and time blocks before sharing widely.
- This does not automatically send a WhatsApp message after each booking; it creates
  a WhatsApp share action so you can distribute the booking-page link. Automated
  WhatsApp notifications require a WhatsApp Business Platform integration.
